I, the undersigned, will use the following accounts to approve documents and
records in the Libre Systems quality management system:

| System | Account |
|---|---|
| GitHub | |
| Google Workspace | |

I understand and agree that:

1. My approval in these systems (a pull-request merge or approving review,
   or an electronic signature on a record) is the **legally binding
   equivalent of my handwritten signature**.
2. I will use only my own accounts, never share my credentials or second
   factor, and keep two-factor authentication turned on.
3. I will state the meaning of each approval (for example "Approved for
   use" or "Approved for release") and approve only content I have reviewed.
4. I will report at once any suspected loss or misuse of my credentials.
5. I will not use any other account I control to approve or merge QMS
   changes.
